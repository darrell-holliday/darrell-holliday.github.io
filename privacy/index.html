Privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This page explains, in plain English, how this website handles your information. It is not legal advice. The business behind the site is Invictus Digital Consulting LLC, a Florida limited liability company. The site is darrellholliday.com. The Coach you’ll deal with is Darrell Holliday. Questions: workwithdarrellh@gmail.com.

What this covers

It covers information you send through this website: the form to request the free playbook, the Ask Workshop waitlist, and calls you book through this site’s Cal.com links. It does not cover a signed coaching agreement, which may have its own terms, or any other site or app I don’t operate.

What I collect

I only collect what you type into a form on this site or on a booking page linked from it.

  • Playbook requests: your email address, and your first name if you give it.
  • Call bookings: your name, email address, the time you choose, and your answers to the booking questions about your business, your offer and what you want help with.
  • Paid calls: payment details go to Stripe. I see the payment status and the name and email on it, not your full card number.
  • Workshop waitlist: your email address, and your first name if you give it.

If you email me directly, I’ll have whatever you include in that email. This website itself does not ask for payment details; paid calls are paid on Stripe’s checkout through Cal.com. I do not run an account system, and I do not collect sensitive categories of information here on purpose.

How I use it

  • to send the playbook, if you asked for it
  • to hold the call you booked and prepare for it
  • to email you workshop dates, price and updates, if you joined the waitlist
  • to keep a simple record of our conversation

I do not sell your information. I do not share it with advertising networks, and I do not add you to a third-party mailing list without asking. You can ask to come off the waitlist at any time.

Who else sees it

Forms on this site are delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). When you submit a form, the contents go to FormSubmit so they can be emailed to workwithdarrellh@gmail.com. FormSubmit’s own privacy terms apply to that transfer. After you submit, FormSubmit may show a spam check (Google reCAPTCHA) on its own pages.

Calls are booked through Cal.com (cal.com), which stores your booking and answers and sends confirmations and reminders. Payments for paid calls are processed by Stripe (stripe.com). Calls take place on Google Meet. Each of these services has its own privacy terms.

I may also see your information in my email inbox, and I may keep a copy while we are talking, or if we work together. If the law requires it, or if I need professional help (for example an accountant or lawyer) to run the practice, I may share what is needed for that purpose. If I later move my mailing list to an email service, I will update this page first.

Cookies and analytics

Fonts are hosted on this site, not pulled from a third-party font service. The site itself does not set advertising cookies.

A short notice asks before any analytics tag loads. If you say yes, Google Analytics (GA4) uses cookies to tell me which pages and buttons people use. No advertising networks. You can say no, and I will remember that choice in your browser. To change your choice, clear this site’s data in your browser and the notice will appear again.

The pages are served by GitHub Pages. That host may keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address and the page requested) to operate the site.

How long I keep it

Requests, waitlist entries and messages are kept long enough to send what you asked for, reply, and keep a reasonable record of the practice. If you want something deleted, email me and I will do what I reasonably can, including in my inbox, unless I have a legal reason to keep it.

Your choices

You can email workwithdarrellh@gmail.com to ask what I have from you, to correct it, or to ask me to delete it. You can also choose not to submit a form. If you are in a place that gives you additional privacy rights, I will honor a request I can reasonably fulfill.

Children

This site is for adults who are building a business. It is not aimed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this explanation changes in a meaningful way, I will update this page and the date at the top.
