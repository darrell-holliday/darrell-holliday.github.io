In a session

Find the step where people stop

Pull up the last post that got attention. Where did you send people from there? We follow what actually happened. If people made it to a call, we listen to the part where you explained the offer. If nobody got that far, we look earlier. We find the step that’s holding the rest back and fix that one first. Every session ends with one next step you read back to me.

  1. The offer

    Tell me who you’d offer it to and what they’d get for their money. We put the package and your reason for the price into words you’d use on a call.

  2. What you say

    Bring a post or an email you’re about to send. We rewrite the part where your reader has to guess what you mean or what to do next.

  3. Funnel design

    We draw the route someone takes from your content to a booking. You can see where a page belongs, which email sends them there and what you’re asking them to do.

  4. Conversion

    I use conversion rate optimization (CRO) to decide what to change. If people click through and leave at the booking page, I want to read that page before we touch the ad.

  5. Leading the sales call

    Start with what brought them to the call. We work on the question you skipped, the recommendation you’d make and the words you’ll use to ask for a decision. If you work with setters, we write their opening script together.

  6. The follow-up

    We write a follow-up around something the person actually said. Beside their name, put what you agreed to do and when to check back.

Before you book

Who do you work with?

I work with online entrepreneurs who sell their own offers, including consultants, creators, Coaches and service providers. You can bring a rough idea, even if you’ve never taken a sales call.

Can I bring a marketing email or a page?

Yes. Bring your content, a landing page or the emails that follow it. I’ll look at what you ask people to do there and how that fits with the conversation you want to have.

Do you guarantee an outcome?

I can give you a close read of your work and a specific change to try. I don’t promise sales, income or any other outcome. Your effort, offer and market all matter. See the earnings disclaimer.

How do I apply for 1:1 coaching?

Start with the Fit Call application. Tell me what you sell and where you’re stuck. If we work together, we use your actual pages and recordings. Before we finish a session, you read back what you’ll do next and tell me when you’ll show me.

Where do I see the call fee?

Choose the Teardown or the Intensive and you’ll see the fee on Cal.com before you pay. Both are non-refundable. I credit the fee toward 1:1 coaching if you join within 14 days of your paid call. The Fit Call is free. Compare the calls.

Can I start without an audience?

Yes. Think of someone who’s already asked you for help, or a colleague who knows people with this problem. We can work on an offer you’d feel comfortable sending to that person.

What hours do you work?

I work from Thailand on US Eastern hours, and every call is on Google Meet.

How do I hear about workshop dates?

I send the dates and terms by email before enrollment opens. You can decide then. Join the Ask Workshop waitlist.
