The short version

I learned to coach from the closer’s chair

I started in growth marketing at a software company: launches, funnels, email, an affiliate program, the user community. Then I moved to the front line and spent years on high-ticket sales calls over Zoom, across software, education and coaching offers.

I trained setters and newer closers and wrote scripts teams used. Somewhere in those calls I noticed that the skill that made me a good closer was the same one that makes a good coach: find out why someone showed up, tell them the truth, and help them make a clear decision.

Now I’m a Sales Coach for online entrepreneurs: coaches, consultants, creators and service providers who sell their own offer. Many build with AI and already have plenty of tools. What they usually need help with is asking, so we start there.

How I work

Constraint first, then one next step

No big framework to buy into. Every session follows the same three moves.

  1. Find the one constraint

    We open your actual offer, messages and call notes, and work out whether the gap is attention, conversations or the ask. Then we work on that, not on everything at once.

  2. Agree one next step

    One action, a date you say yes to, and how you’ll show me it’s done. Small enough that you’ll actually do it before we talk again.

  3. Read it back

    You say the step back in your own words before we hang up. If it doesn’t sound clear out loud, we fix it then.

Questions people ask

Who do you work with?

Online entrepreneurs who sell their own offer: coaches, consultants, creators and service providers. Many are building with AI tools. You don’t need sales experience, and you don’t need to be technical.

Is this sales coaching or business coaching?

Sales coaching for people who sell their own offer. We work on the offer itself, who you talk to, what you say on calls and how you follow up. Marketing and AI tools come up when they affect the ask.

Do you promise results?

No. I don’t promise income, sales or any outcome. What happens depends on your effort, your offer and your market. What I can promise is honest feedback and a clear next step each time we talk. Read the earnings disclaimer.

How does 1:1 sales coaching work?

Twelve weeks, one call a week by video call, with messages between calls for quick questions. Each call ends with one next step you’ve read back to me. The price is set on the Fit Call once we both know what the work is.

What do the calls cost?

The Fit Call is free and takes 15 minutes. The Sales Call Teardown is $197 for 60 minutes, and the Offer & Ask Intensive is $497 for 90 minutes. Paid calls are non-refundable, and the fee is credited toward 1:1 sales coaching if you join within 14 days. See the call options.

Do I need an audience first?

No. The playbook starts with people you can already reach: past clients, colleagues, friends of friends and people in communities you’re part of.
