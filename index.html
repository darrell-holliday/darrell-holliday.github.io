Inside the PDF

  1. Four checks for choosing one idea. Who it’s for, what changes for them, why now, and ten people it fits.

  2. An offer paragraph with blanks to fill in. Read it aloud and cut anything you’d never say to a friend.

  3. Make a list of twenty people you can reach without ads. Write how you’ll contact each one.

  4. A first message, four call questions and a follow-up. One question asks what the person has already tried.

  5. What to leave alone for now, and where AI can help: draft with it, then read every word before it goes out.
