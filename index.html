The short version

I learned coaching from the other side of the sales call

I started in growth marketing at a software company: launches, funnels, email, an affiliate program, the user community. Then I moved to the front line and spent years on high-ticket sales calls over Zoom, across software, education and coaching offers.

I trained setters and newer closers and wrote scripts teams used. Somewhere in those calls I noticed that the skill that made me a good closer was the same one that makes a good coach: find out why someone showed up, tell them the truth, and help them make a clear decision.

Now I coach online entrepreneurs who are building with AI. Most of them don’t need more tools or more modules. They need one offer, a list of real people, the nerve to ask, and someone steady in their corner while they do it.

How I work

Constraint first, then one next step

No big framework to buy into. Every session follows the same three moves.

  1. Find the one constraint

    We open your actual page, offer and messages, and work out whether the gap is attention, conversations or the ask. Then we work on that, not on everything at once.

  2. Agree one next step

    One action, a date you say yes to, and how you’ll show me it’s done. Small enough that you’ll actually do it before we talk again.

  3. Read it back

    You say the step back in your own words before we hang up. If it doesn’t sound clear out loud, we fix it then.

Questions people ask

Who do you work with?

Online entrepreneurs: coaches, consultants, creators and service providers who are building their business with AI tools and want help with the offer and the conversations around it. You don’t need to be technical.

Do you promise results?

No. I don’t promise income, sales or any outcome. What happens depends on your effort, your offer and your market. What I can promise is honest feedback and a clear next step each time we talk. Read the earnings disclaimer.

How does 1:1 coaching work?

Twelve weeks, one call a week over Zoom, with messages between calls for quick questions. Each call ends with one next step you’ve read back to me. The price is set on the Strategy Call once we both know what the work is.

What does the Strategy Call cost?

Nothing. It’s a conversation about your business and whether coaching is a fit. If it isn’t, I’ll tell you, and often point you to something better suited.

Do I need an audience first?

No. The playbook starts with people you can already reach: past clients, colleagues, friends of friends and people in communities you’re part of.

Where are you based?

I’m from College Park, Maryland, and I work from Thailand on US Eastern hours.
