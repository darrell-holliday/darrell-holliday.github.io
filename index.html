Questions I get a lot

Who is this for?

Online entrepreneurs (coaches, consultants, creators, service providers) who are building with AI but have not made consistent money yet. If you've learned a lot and sold a little, that's the gap I work in.

What happens in the 12 weeks?

Weekly one-to-one calls, plus quick messages between sessions. Weeks 1 to 6: one offer, a 90-day number, and first sales from real conversations. Weeks 7 to 12: simple AI-assisted systems around what's already working.

Do you guarantee a specific income?

No. I will not promise a number. Anyone who does is guessing, or selling you a story. The promise is a clear offer, a sales routine, and real conversations every week.

I already have a lot of AI tools. Does that matter?

Treat them like interns, not employees. We use AI once an offer is already selling, to draft and speed up work you would do anyway. We don't start by building a machine for something nobody has bought.
