About

I built the funnels. I took the calls.

I’m Darrell Holliday, a Sales & Marketing Coach for online entrepreneurs. I’ve written the launch email and I’ve been the closer waiting on Zoom, so I know what each side of that handoff sees.

How I got here

I grew up in College Park, Maryland, and studied philosophy at Arizona State. It’s useful on a call to notice when you’ve answered a different question from the one somebody asked.

I worked in growth marketing at Funnelytics, a funnel-analytics software company. I worked on launches, with affiliates and in the user community. When I moved into sales for its agency coaching program, I got to talk to the people who’d come through those funnels.

I also ran Offer Launch Labs, my own small consultancy. A client could bring me an offer idea and I’d work on the positioning, write the copy and build the funnel for the launch.

I closed $12M+ in sales over Zoom across software, education and coaching offers. I trained in NEPQ, SPIN and Remote Closing Academy methods, trained setters and wrote scripts sales teams used. When we review your script, I look at the opening question as well as the part where you ask someone to buy.

Why coaching

As a closer I had to understand why someone had shown up before I could recommend anything. I still start there. When someone says they need a new funnel, I ask to see where the current one sends people. Sometimes the page does its job and the part we need to work on is what they say after someone books.

I’ve coached 1,000+ people. At an online business-education company, I run daily live office hours, teach live workshops on building with AI and lead a team of Coaches. Part of my job is reviewing their calls and listening for whether the client understood what to do next.

I work directly with online entrepreneurs from Thailand on US Eastern hours. If we rewrite a section of your page together, I’ll ask you to talk me through the edit. I want you to be able to do it yourself after the call.

What it’s like to work with me

  • Find the one constraint first. Traffic, the page, the booking or the ask: one of them is holding the rest back, so we start there.
  • Recommending the right thing isn’t salesy. When it fits, I say so plainly and let them decide.
  • Ten names on paper beat a perfect avatar in your head. We start your offer with people you could message today.
  • Keep your call notes. Write down what the person said when you asked. I’d want that in front of us before we changed the script.
  • Bring the rough draft. You learn by doing, and I’m patient with beginners.

Outside of work

I like martial arts, lifting and chess. In any of them, there’s usually something pretty basic I could stand to practise again.
