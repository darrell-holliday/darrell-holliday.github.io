Paid calls: all sales are final

The Sales Call Teardown ($197) and the Offer & Ask Intensive ($497) are paid in full when you book, through Stripe. These fees are non-refundable. Please check the date, time and time zone before you pay.

  • One free reschedule. You can move a paid call once, at no cost, as long as you do it at least 24 hours before the start time. Use the reschedule link in your Cal.com confirmation email.
  • Inside 24 hours. A call that is cancelled, moved or missed less than 24 hours before its start time is not refunded and can’t be rescheduled.
  • If I need to move a call. I’ll offer you a new time at no cost, and it doesn’t count as your reschedule.

Credit toward coaching

If you join 1:1 sales coaching within 14 days of your Sales Call Teardown or Offer & Ask Intensive, the fee you paid is credited toward your coaching fee. The credit applies once per paid call, can’t be exchanged for cash, and expires after the 14 days.
