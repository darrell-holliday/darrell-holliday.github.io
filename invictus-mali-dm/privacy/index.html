Privacy Policy — Invictus Mali DM

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Last Updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Invictus Digital Consulting LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and deletes information in connection with the Invictus Mali DM Meta application (the "App").

Contact: consultinginvictus@gmail.com

1. Purpose of the App

Invictus Mali DM operates exclusively for Instagram and Facebook Page messaging workflows owned or managed by Invictus Digital Consulting LLC (specifically the Instagram professional account @silvermali.moves and its linked Facebook Page Mali | Silver Fitness 60+). When a user comments a requested keyword (such as GLOW) on a post or sends a keyword Direct Message, the App responds via official Meta Graph API endpoints to verify whether the user follows the account and, if confirmed, delivers the requested informational or affiliate link along with required disclosures.

2. Information We Process

When you interact with our Instagram account or Facebook Page via comments or Direct Messages, Meta Platforms, Inc. ("Meta") sends webhook events to our App containing only the minimum data required to respond:

  • Identifiers: Your Instagram-scoped user ID (IGSID), comment ID, media/post ID, and Instagram username (if returned by Meta's API).
  • Interaction Content: The text of the comment or Direct Message you send to trigger or continue the automated conversation (for example, the keyword GLOW).
  • Follow Status Flag: The boolean is_user_follow_business field returned by Meta's User Profile API only after you have initiated messaging or interacted inside Direct Messages (user consent per Meta's platform rules).
  • Operational Metadata: Timestamps, webhook event IDs, and which reply template variant was sent (used solely to prevent duplicate messages and rotate response wording).

We do not collect payment card numbers, government IDs, passwords, sensitive health records, or third-party browsing histories through this App.

3. How We Use Information

We use the data above strictly to:

  • Respond to user-initiated comments and Direct Messages with the requested follow-up prompt or link.
  • Verify follower status via Meta's official is_user_follow_business field before delivering follower-gated links.
  • Prevent duplicate replies to the same comment or message (idempotency) and avoid sending identical wording back-to-back.
  • Maintain basic security, webhook signature verification, and error troubleshooting logs.

We do not sell your personal data, rent it to data brokers, or use it to build third-party advertising profiles.

4. Data Sharing & Service Providers

All comment and Direct Message transport occurs through Meta Platforms, Inc. in accordance with Meta's Privacy Policy and Platform Terms. We may use standard cloud infrastructure providers (such as serverless HTTPS webhook hosting and encrypted database/KV storage) solely to host the App's webhook endpoint and operational state. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law, subpoena, or valid legal process.

5. Data Retention

We retain operational identifiers (such as processed comment_id values, IGSID hashes or IDs, and last-sent message variant markers) only for as long as necessary to prevent duplicate replies, manage message rotation, and diagnose technical issues—generally no longer than 90 days unless a user requests earlier deletion or a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data processed by Invictus Mali DM at any time by following our User Data Deletion Instructions or emailing consultinginvictus@gmail.com. We process verified deletion requests within 30 days (and automated Meta data-deletion callbacks immediately upon receipt).

7. Children's Privacy

This App and its associated social media pages are intended for adults and are not directed to individuals under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
