User Data Deletion Instructions — Invictus Mali DM

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Operator: Invictus Digital Consulting LLC (Florida, USA)

Invictus Mali DM respects your privacy and provides two straightforward ways to delete any data associated with your Instagram or Facebook interaction with our App.

Option 1: Request Deletion by Email (Recommended for Direct Support)

  1. Send an email to consultinginvictus@gmail.com with the subject line: Data Deletion Request — Invictus Mali DM.
  2. Include your Instagram handle (e.g., @yourusername) and approximate date of the comment or Direct Message so we can locate any stored operational record.
  3. We will permanently delete any stored identifiers (such as your IGSID, comment deduplication record, and template rotation marker) from our App database and logs, and confirm completion by email within 30 days (typically within 48–72 hours).

Option 2: Revoke Access & Automated Meta Data Deletion

If you previously authorized or interacted with a Meta app through your Facebook or Instagram settings:

  1. Open your Facebook or Instagram Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites (or Business Integrations).
  2. Locate Invictus Mali DM and select Remove.
  3. When Meta sends a signed data-deletion callback for your user ID, our App removes any corresponding operational records linked to that ID and returns a confirmation code.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Any stored Instagram-scoped user ID (IGSID) or hashed user identifier.
  • Processed comment IDs and message-variant rotation counters linked to your ID.
  • Any temporary troubleshooting logs referencing your interaction.

Note: Removing data from our App deletes records stored by Invictus Digital Consulting LLC. Messages and comments visible inside Instagram itself remain governed by Instagram/Meta's native platform controls and can also be deleted directly inside the Instagram app.
