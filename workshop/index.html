What we do in each session

Each session is live on Zoom, recorded, and ends with one thing you do before the next one. The workbook has a page for each.

  1. Write the offer

    Who it’s for, what they get and what it costs, in one paragraph you can say out loud.

  2. List the people

    Twenty names you can reach today, without ads: past clients, colleagues and communities you’re already in.

  3. Open the conversation

    A first message that sounds like you, and how to send it without feeling pushy.

  4. Run the call

    The questions I ask, how to recommend clearly, and how to let the other person decide.

  5. Follow up

    What to send after a call, after a “not now” and after silence, and how to keep a simple record.
