What you’ll put on paper

We meet online and I record each session. Bring your workbook. You’ll use what you write on each page for a small assignment before we meet again.

  1. Say what you sell

    Write the offer in a paragraph, price included. Read it aloud so we can hear where it needs work.

  2. Name your people

    Make a list of twenty names. Include people who’ve asked for help and people who could introduce you.

  3. Send the first message

    Write to someone on your list. Start with the honest reason that person came to mind.

  4. Make the ask

    We practise what comes after your recommendation. Give the person room to answer when you ask, “Is this something you’d like to do?”

  5. Check back

    Use a detail from your notes to write a follow-up. Decide when you’ll send it and where to record their answer.
