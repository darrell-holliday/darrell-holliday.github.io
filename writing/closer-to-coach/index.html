I sold about $12M over Zoom. Then I stopped selling and started coaching.

For years my job was the close.

Software. Education. Coaching programs from $5K to $50K. Somewhere around $12M in lifetime sales, most of it on Zoom calls with people who were scared to spend the money and more scared to stay where they were.

I was good at it. I also built the funnels, the offers, the pricing and the follow-up emails. If you’ve ever bought a high-ticket program online, there’s a decent chance someone like me was on the other end of that call.

And then I burned out.

Not dramatically. I just noticed I felt empty after a big week. The number went up and I didn’t care. I kept thinking about the people I’d enrolled. Did they actually do the thing? Did it work? I had no idea. My job ended when they paid.

So I moved to the other side of the table. I started coaching.

Here’s what surprised me. Every skill I learned closing works even better after the sale.

Tie-downs become commitments. "So if we get this done by Friday, you’re ready to launch, right?"

Future pacing becomes the reason people keep going in week three, when the excitement is gone and the work is boring.

Reframes are how you talk someone off the ledge when their first launch makes $0.

The difference is who it’s for. On a sales call, those tools serve the company. In coaching, they serve the person in front of you.

I’ve now coached 350+ people building online businesses, most of them using AI to do it. The ones who get paid aren’t the ones with the best tools. They’re the ones who sell something specific, to someone specific, and keep showing up when the excitement fades.

That’s what I help with now. Sales is where my credibility comes from. Coaching is the work I actually want to do.

If you’re building something online and you’re stuck between "I’ve learned a lot" and "I’ve made money," that gap is my favorite place to work.

Darrell Holliday

Business coach and consultant. I sold $12M+ over Zoom, then coached 350+ people building online businesses. I help online entrepreneurs sell one offer, then use AI on what’s already working.
